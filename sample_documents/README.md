# A tiny, permitted library

The three PDFs here are original fictional teaching materials released under **CC0-1.0**. You may copy, share, and adapt them. Their study results are invented fixtures, not scientific findings.

Upload all three PDFs in the dashboard. No large download or external dataset is required. `corpus.json` contains their page-by-page source text; `generate.py` reproduces the PDFs with ReportLab.

Try “What is the Canopy project research question?”, “What is the mean comfort difference?”, or “How long are raw coded observations retained?” Then ask “Who funded the Canopy project?” to test abstention.

For your own 3–5 sample documents, use self-authored notes, openly licensed papers, or documents with explicit sharing permission. Prefer short, text-based PDFs. Keep the license and original source URL in a small manifest. Avoid personal or confidential data. Upload each file, wait for Ready, and verify a known fact on a known physical page before evaluating the library. Additional documents can change retrieval and invalidate the supplied expected results.
